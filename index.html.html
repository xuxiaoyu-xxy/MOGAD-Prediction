    Model and Validation Methods

    The multivariable logistic regression model was fitted in the derivation cohort using eight categorical predictors and MOGAD diagnosis as the outcome. The probability threshold was selected by maximizing the Youden index. A prediction at or above the threshold is model-positive.

    Internal and external validation used the derivation coefficients and the unrounded probability threshold. The model includes repeat serology among its predictors. Complete all eight entries when the required findings are available.

    Validation retained the original cohort coding. Among records with source variable Clinical_phenotype1 coded as 0, optic neuritis laterality was coded as 1 for 0 of 187 in derivation, 93 of 94 in internal validation, and 66 of 67 in external validation.

    OCB denotes oligoclonal bands. FCBA denotes fixed cell-based assay. The 1:32 cut point defines the titer predictor in this model. Use the laboratory’s assay-specific criteria to determine low-positive status.

    Clinical context and evidence

    Low-Positive MOG-IgG

    Use the reporting laboratory’s assay-specific interpretation. For low-positive serum MOG-IgG results, the 2023 criteria require a compatible core clinical demyelinating event, at least one supporting clinical or MRI feature, negative aquaporin-4 IgG (AQP4-IgG), and exclusion of a better diagnosis.

    Banwell et al. · Diagnostic criteria Sechi et al. · Titer and positive predictive value

    TRUE-MOGAD Score

    Vilaseca et al. developed a separate point-based score in a cohort that included both low-positive and clear-positive MOG-IgG results. The published classification threshold for the TRUE-MOGAD score is ≥2 points.
